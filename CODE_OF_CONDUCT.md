# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus discussion on the project. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate confidentiality.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
